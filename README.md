## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms

The cloud desktop thin client management market spans **hardware-bundled solutions** (Amazon WorkSpaces Thin Client) that ship pre-configured devices directly to end users, and **software-defined endpoint management platforms** (IGEL, Stratodesk, Dell Wyse) that standardize and manage heterogeneous hardware fleets. **Amazon WorkSpaces Thin Client** is **priced at $195 per device**, ships directly from Amazon fulfillment centers, and supports setup in minutes without IT assistance . **IGEL** transitioned to a **subscription license model (ISL)** starting January 1, 2026, replacing the Workspace Edition License for new purchases . **Stratodesk NoTouch** is **hardware-agnostic**, running on any x86 or ARM-based device including Raspberry Pis, with **NoTouch Center** capable of running on-premises or from the cloud . **Dell Wyse Management Suite** offers a **free Standard edition** with a one-year subscription license for small deployments, while Pro supports up to **hundreds of thousands of devices** . **HP Anyware Manager** is included with Anyware Standard and Professional subscriptions, available as SaaS or installable for full control .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Epoptes](https://github.com/epoptes/epoptes)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/epoptes/epoptes?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/epoptes/epoptes/stargazers)  
  **Open-source computer lab management and monitoring tool**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most widely deployed open-source thin client management solution** — translated into **40+ languages** and used in installations worldwide . **Screen broadcasting and monitoring** for instructors. **Remote command execution** and **message sending** to clients. **Impose restrictions** like screen locking or sound muting. Works with **LTSP servers, thin and fat clients, standalone workstations, FreeRDP, and X2Go** clients. Installs on **Ubuntu, Debian, and openSUSE** . **The standard open-source tool for managing thin client labs in educational environments** . 🎓

- **[endpoint-aiops](https://github.com/endpoint-aiops/endpoint-aiops)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/endpoint-aiops/endpoint-aiops?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/endpoint-aiops/endpoint-aiops/stargazers)  
  **Governed AI-ops for managed endpoint fleets**, MIT licensed. **Vendor-neutral** — talks to any endpoint-management server's REST API via Bearer auth . **Login-storm analysis** — detects concurrent login bursts during "everyone logs in at 9am" incidents and ranks endpoints dragging login times. **Patch/config drift detection** — finds endpoints that drifted from fleet baseline, deriving the baseline by fleet majority when no gold image exists. **Encrypted credentials** stored in `~/.endpoint-aiops/secrets.enc` (Fernet + scrypt). **Reversibility** — `endpoint_assign_profile` captures prior profile and records undo descriptor. **Safety** — state-changing ops require double confirmation and support `--dry-run`. **Read-only mode** (`ENDPOINT_READ_ONLY=1`) removes write tools entirely from the MCP session — not hidden, not gated, **absent** . **The most modern open-source endpoint management AI-ops tool** . 🤖

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Amazon WorkSpaces Thin Client reaches End of Support on March 31, 2027** — existing customers can continue using the service until that date, but **no new customers accepted after April 20, 2026** . AWS recommends transitioning to supported alternatives from partners .
- **IGEL Cloud Gateway requirements vary by OS version**: **IGEL OS 12 devices may not require ICG** depending on use case, but **ICG 12 is required** for remote OS 11 device management . Minimum server requirements: **4 GB RAM, 2 CPUs, 2 GB free space**.
- **Dell Wyse Management Suite Standard** is free for **one year** on registration — not a permanent free tier. **Pro supports up to hundreds of thousands of devices** but requires per-seat licensing .
- **Epoptes is designed for educational computer labs** — it provides screen broadcasting, remote command execution, and restrictions, but is **not a general-purpose enterprise thin client management platform**. For enterprise fleet management with firmware updates and asset tracking, commercial solutions remain necessary .
- **endpoint-aiops is a community-maintained project** — the endpoint-management REST paths have **not been exercised against a live management server**. Test suite is mock-based. See `docs/VERIFICATION.md` for details .
- Open-source solutions (Epoptes, endpoint-aiops) provide self-hosted ownership and transparency, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees, multi-vendor hardware support, and 24/7 support remain primarily commercial offerings. **Always validate management operations against a test fleet before applying to production**. 🖥️
